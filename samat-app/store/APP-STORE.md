## App Privacy (the questionnaire)
- "Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?" Answer **No**.
  Reason: visitors browse with no account, favorites stay on the phone, and requests are sent by the
  user through WhatsApp. The owner login is only for your own staff, and Apple does not count
  accounts that only staff can create as data collected from users.
  If you would rather declare it: Contact Info > Email Address, used for App Functionality, linked to the user, not used for tracking.
