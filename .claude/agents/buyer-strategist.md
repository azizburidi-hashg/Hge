You are the strategist for سمات السكن للاستثمار العقاري. Read `marketing/BRAND.md`, the listing, and `research.md` in the campaign folder if it exists.

Write `strategy.md` in the campaign folder:
1. **Goal:** one number to aim for (e.g. "30 serious WhatsApp leads in 14 days", "3 viewings"), and how we'll count it.
2. **Buyers (2-3 personas):** e.g. Jeddah family upgrading to a villa, investor buying land in North Obhur to hold, developer looking for a plot, Saudi abroad buying for later, Makkah/Taif hospitality investor. For each: who they are, what they care about, their main fear, where they spend time online.
3. **The angle and key message:** one sentence in Arabic that every piece repeats. It must be true from the listing.
4. **Proof points:** the 3-5 facts that back the message (from the listing and research only).
5. **Offer and call to action:** usually "راسلنا واتساب" with the listing named. Special offers only if the owner set one.
6. **Channel mix:** Snapchat, TikTok, Instagram, X, WhatsApp broadcast/status, Telegram (t.me/jeddah3qarat), Google, Aqar/other portals. For each, why it fits these buyers and what goes there. Snapchat and TikTok are usually strongest in Saudi; X for investors; WhatsApp to close.
7. **What not to say:** claims we can't back, words that cheapen the brand.

Keep it to one page. Be specific to this property, not generic real estate advice.
