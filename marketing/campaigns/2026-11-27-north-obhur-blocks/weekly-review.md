## 3. What to change when a number is low
| Low number | Likely reason | What to change next week (draft for the owner to approve) |
|---|---|---|
| **Qualified chats below pace** (fewer than 4 by day 7) | Too few of the right people reached | Put the effort where the leads are. Ask the owner for more developer, investor and broker contacts he knows, for one wave 1 message each (copy.md section 7). Don't send a third message to people who already got two. Post on X more for investors. Cut the weakest social slot. |
| Chats come in but **aren't qualified** | Price shoppers, or families | Use the leads.md section 2 questions early. Lead with "بلوك كامل" and HOOK-8 or HOOK-10 for developers. Drop the reach-style hooks. |
| **Qualified, but no meeting or visit** | Missing pin, visit times or documents | Close the owner's open items: pin and visit times (leads.md items 6 and 16), deed and zoning (items 4 and 5). Offer a short call first (leads.md section 5). |
| Outreach **replies low** (under about 20% of messages sent) | Wrong contacts, or the message feels generic | Personalise the first line. Check that each contact really builds or buys land. Use the block size that fits them (WA-DEV-3 lets them choose). |
| **Reach low** on a social channel | Bad time slot, or weak first frame | Move it to the other good window (13:00 to 14:00 or 20:00 to 23:30, Thursday night to Saturday). Try a different HOOK from copy.md. Post when the account usually gets activity. No boosting. |
| **Views high, profile visits low** | The hook works but the video doesn't make people want more | Put the key message («بلوك كامل … أربعة شوارع 20 م … 3,300 ريال للمتر») in the first 3 seconds. Shorten the video (use the 18 s single-block cuts). |
| **Profile visits high, link or WhatsApp clicks low** | The profile doesn't lead anywhere | Put the WhatsApp link first in the bio, pin X-1, keep the IG highlight "بلوك 32 و35" at the front, and use the link sticker on every story. |
| **Clicks high, few chats** | Friction after the click | Test wa.me/966555282922 on a phone. Check the property page loads and shows the FAL number and the ad license numbers. Make sure someone replies within minutes (leads.md section 1). |
| **Many "unknown source" leads** | The source question wasn't asked | Ask «من وين عرفت عن العرض؟» in every first reply. Optional, with the owner's OK: a different prefilled wa.me message per channel. |
| A channel brings **zero leads after 2 weeks** | Not where these buyers are | Keep it at a minimum (1 post a week) and move the time to WhatsApp direct and X, which the strategy ranks first. |
| **Videos not ready** | Footage, 3D or approval still pending | Use the photo fallbacks in calendar.md. Don't swap in stock or AI footage. List what's blocking each video for the owner. |
