# Strategy: بلوك 32 وبلوك 35، منطقة عمائر أبحر الشمالية

Free / organic only (no paid ads, no boosting). 14 days: 2026-11-28 to 2026-12-11. Sources: `listing.md`, `research.md`, `../../BRAND.md`.

## 1. Goal
**8 qualified buyer conversations on WhatsApp in 14 days, with at least 2 of them becoming a meeting or site visit.**
- A conversation is "qualified" when the person names the block (32 or 35), says who is buying (themselves, a company, a fund or a partnership), and asks for a next step: deed or zoning details, the location pin, a meeting, or a visit. "كم السعر؟" on its own does not count.
- How we count: the owner labels each WhatsApp chat `بلوك 32` / `بلوك 35`, asks every new contact "من وين عرفت عن العرض؟", and logs it in `leads.md` in this folder (date, name or company, block, source channel, qualified yes/no, next step). We review on day 7 and day 14.
- Why so few: at about 25.7M and 46.2M SAR per block, one right buyer is the whole job. Reach and views don't matter here. Only qualified talks do.

## 7. What not to say
- Never "أقل من سعر السوق", "فرصة لا تتكرر", "أفضل سعر", "لقطة" or "حرق أسعار". The only allowed comparison is "أقل من أسعار العرض المنشورة للقطع المنفردة".
- No returns, yields, expected price rises or "سيتضاعف" claims.
- Don't give distances or opening dates for Jeddah Tower, the drainage projects or Prince Mishaal bin Majed Road, and don't say the blocks are near them unless the owner confirms.
- Don't say how many floors are allowed, the deed type, that single plots can be bought separately, or the size of each plot. These are unknown. Ask the owner.
- Don't say who pays the 5% transaction tax, and don't mention white land fees in ads. If a buyer asks, the owner answers.
- No family, villa or "سكن العمر" language. No pressure lines like "آخر فرصة" or "العدد محدود".
- No stock images or 3D "proposed project" pictures shown as if they were real.

**Owner to confirm before launch:** the REGA ad license number for each block, whole block only or single plots too, deed type, floors allowed, the location pin, and whether we work with other brokers.
