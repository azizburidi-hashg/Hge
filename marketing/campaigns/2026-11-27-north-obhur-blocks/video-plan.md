### Problems found in the photos (I looked at both images)
The two "photos" are **designed flyers, not site photos**. Each one has three parts: an aerial city image at the top, a colored plot plan in the middle, and an info strip with the logo at the bottom. Both are 862 to 865 x 1280 px.
- **The aerial backgrounds don't look like real photos of the site.** The Block 35 flyer shows a sea bay and a tall-tower skyline, and the Block 32 flyer shows a generic villa district. Treat them as illustrations. Any shot that shows them carries a small label **«صورة توضيحية»**, and the voiceover never says "this is the view from the land". The brand rule is "no fake luxury stock that isn't the property".
- **The plot plans are diagrams**, not surveyed plans. Shots that show them carry **«مخطط توضيحي»**.
- **The Block 32 plan doesn't match the listing.** Its single row of 16 plots reads 229, 228, 223, 224, 225, 226, 227, **228**, 219 ... 212. That means 228 appears twice and 220, 221 and 222 are missing. The listing says plots 212 to 229, which is 18 numbers. The Block 35 flyer draws the same Block 32 as two rows (212 to 220 and 221 to 229).
- **The Block 32 flyer labels a street "شارع عرض 32 متر" with the tag «الشمالي».** The listing and the flyer's own info strip say all four streets are 20 m. On the Block 35 flyer, the 32 m street is north of plots 73 to 82, and Block 32 itself has a 20 m street. A buyer could read the 32 m label as Block 32's street.
- **Both plans draw a mosque (مسجد) next to the blocks.** That isn't in the listing. The voiceover and text don't mention it unless the owner confirms.
- **Resolution:** to fill a 1080x1920 frame the flyers are scaled up about 1.5x, and a close-up on the plan is 3x or more, which will look soft. **Ask the owner for the original high-resolution design files** (and a corrected Block 32 plan) before editing.
- Until the Block 32 plan is fixed, Block 32 shots **stay wide and don't zoom on the plot numbers.** The alternative is the 3D streets diagram (shot 3D-4), which is labeled as an illustration.

### Production rules for all three
- **Editor:** a free editor the owner already uses (CapCut, InShot or similar) for the cuts, Arabic text, voiceover and music. No connected or paid video, voice or AI-generation service is used. If the owner later wants one, the price quote comes first and the owner says yes in chat.
- **Voiceover:** the owner (or a team member) records it on a phone in a quiet room, in calm Saudi white dialect, about 2.5 words per second. No AI voice.
- **On-screen text:** Arabic, big, short (2 to 5 words a line, 2 lines at most), white or gold `#C8A45D` on a navy `#00264D` band at 85% opacity. A free Arabic font from the editor's library (Tajawal or Cairo style, bold). Keep text inside the safe area: away from the top 220 px, the bottom 420 px and the right 140 px, where TikTok, Reels and Snapchat put their buttons.
- **Photo motion:** slow Ken Burns moves (zoom 100% to 115% over 3 s, or a pan), never faster than that, because fast moves on a scaled-up flyer look soft. For Arabic, pans move right to left.
- **Labels:** «صورة توضيحية» or «مخطط توضيحي» in small white text (about 28 px), bottom-left above the safe line, on every flyer shot that shows the aerial image or the plan. 3D shots carry «رسم توضيحي» when they show a diagram.
- **Arabic in Blender:** the Blender Kit's fonts (Montserrat, Anton, Gelasio, Bebas Neue, Share Tech Mono) have **no Arabic letters**, and Blender doesn't join Arabic letters or set them right to left. So the 3D shots carry **only digits, the logo image and shapes**, and the Arabic words are added in the editor on top. Every Blender request below says so.
- **Music:** no copyrighted tracks. Use the platform's own commercial or business library (TikTok Commercial Music Library, Instagram/Meta Sound Collection, Snapchat Sounds), or a royalty-free track whose license the owner has checked (for example the YouTube Audio Library, "no attribution required" filter). Music sits at -18 to -20 dB under the voice.
- **End card (all three videos, last 4 to 5 s):** the logo (`samat-app/brand/logo-transparent.png`) on navy, then
  - واتساب +966 55 528 2922
  - رخصة فال 1100304235
  - رقم ترخيص الإعلان: بلوك 32 [رقم ترخيص الإعلان] · بلوك 35 [رقم ترخيص الإعلان]

  The numbers are typed exactly as written here and checked against BRAND.md before export.
